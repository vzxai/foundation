## Sound

The score is synthesized from scratch in `score/` (numpy, scipy, numba). The spoken and sung voices are espeak-ng speech, vocoded or whispered through the same code. No samples are used.

## Texts quoted or paraphrased on screen

- G. W. F. Hegel, *Science of Logic* (1812): being, nothing, becoming (paraphrase)
- Jürgen Schmidhuber, formal theory of creativity, fun and intrinsic motivation (1990–2010): beauty as compressibility, interest as compression progress (paraphrase)
- Immanuel Kant, *Critique of Judgment* (1790): purposiveness without purpose (paraphrase); *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* (1785): the kingdom of ends (paraphrase)
- Gilles Deleuze and Félix Guattari, *A Thousand Plateaus* (1980): the rhizome (paraphrase)
- Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels, *Manifesto of the Communist Party* (1848; Moore translation 1888): “he becomes an appendage of the machine”
- Nick Land and the CCRU (1990s): hyperstition as “a positive feedback circuit including culture as a component”
- Mark Fisher, *Capitalist Realism* (2009): “it is easier to imagine the end of the world than the end of capitalism” (Fisher attributes it to Jameson and Žižek)
- Stafford Beer: the recursive system theorem of the Viable System Model (*Brain of the Firm*, 1972; *The Heart of Enterprise*, 1979) and “the purpose of a system is what it does”
- Richard Hamming, *Numerical Methods for Scientists and Engineers* (1962): “the purpose of computing is insight, not numbers”
- Eliezer Yudkowsky and Robin Hanson, the AI-foom debate (2008)
